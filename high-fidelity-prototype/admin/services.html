Review checklist

  • Service name and category accurately describe the offering.
  • Description is clear, relevant, and does not make misleading claims.
  • Image is appropriate and represents the service.
  • Duration and price are provided and understandable.
  • Service complies with GlowUp's terms and safety expectations.
