How It Works

Whether you’re booking a service or offering one, here’s what to expect.

For customers

  1. Browse approved services. Compare providers and the services they offer.
  2. Choose an appointment. Review the service details and select an available time.
  3. Manage and review. Keep track of your appointment and share feedback after it’s completed.
Go to sign up →

Choose Customer as your account type.

For providers

  1. Create a provider account. Set up a profile to introduce your services.
  2. Submit your listing for review. Add service details and wait for admin review.
  3. Manage your services. Approved listings become visible to customers so you can manage appointments.
Go to sign up →

Choose Provider as your account type.
