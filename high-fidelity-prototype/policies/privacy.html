Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains how GlowUp uses information provided through the platform.

Information We Collect

GlowUp may collect account details, contact information, profile preferences, provider listings, booking details, and reviews.

How We Use Information

We use this information to create accounts, support bookings, connect customers and providers, and improve the platform.

Information Visibility

Provider profiles, service listings, and submitted reviews may be visible to other GlowUp users.

Information Protection

GlowUp limits access to personal information to the people and systems that need it to operate the platform.

Account Changes

Users may request updates to their account information or ask questions about how their information is used.
