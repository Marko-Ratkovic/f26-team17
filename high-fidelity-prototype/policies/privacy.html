Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how GlowUp uses information provided through the platform.

Information We Collect

GlowUp may collect account details, contact information, profile preferences, optional allergy and sensitivity notes, provider listings, booking details, reviews, provider replies, content reports, and moderation decisions.

How We Use Information

We use this information to create accounts, support bookings, connect customers and providers, support customer-selected preferences, review reports about content, protect platform safety, and improve the platform.

Information Visibility

Provider profiles, service listings, submitted reviews, and provider replies may be visible to other GlowUp users. Customer allergy and sensitivity notes are not public profile information. They should be shared with a provider only in the context of that customer's confirmed appointment. Booking preferences are for customer convenience and are not medical or safety instructions. Reports and moderation notes are available only to administrators who need them to review reported content.

Information Protection

GlowUp limits access to personal information to the people and systems that need it to operate the platform. The live service should enforce access on the server, including access to appointment-specific safety notes.

Account Changes

Users may update their profile information and request account deactivation or deletion. Requests should be confirmed and processed through a documented workflow. Booking, review, report, and moderation records may need to be retained for defined operational or safety purposes; personal account details should be removed or de-identified when they are no longer required.

Retention and Account Requests

GlowUp must define retention periods and explain what happens to existing appointments, reviews, reports, and moderation records before account deletion is available. A deletion request does not mean that every linked record is immediately erased. Exact retention periods and request timelines must be established before launch.
