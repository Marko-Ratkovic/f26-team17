Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how GlowUp uses information provided through the platform.

Information We Collect

GlowUp may collect account details, contact information, saved providers, optional allergy and sensitivity notes, provider listings, booking details, reviews, provider replies, content reports, and moderation decisions.

How We Use Information

We use this information to create accounts, support bookings, connect customers and providers, send account and appointment emails, review reports about content, protect platform safety, and improve the platform.

Information Visibility

Provider profiles, service listings, submitted reviews, and provider replies may be visible to other GlowUp users. Customer allergy and sensitivity notes are not public profile information. They should be shared with a provider only in the context of that customer's confirmed appointment. Reports and moderation notes are available only to administrators who need them to review reported content

Information Protection

GlowUp limits access to personal information to the people and systems that need it to operate the platform. The live service should enforce access on the server, including access to appointment-specific safety notes.

Account Changes

Users may update their profile information or close their account. A closed account can be reopened by logging in within 14 days. After 14 days, personal details such as name, phone, and safety notes are removed, and the account appears as "Deleted user." Booking, review, report, and moderation records are kept.
