Terms of Service

Last updated: September 2026

GlowUp connects customers with beauty and grooming service providers. By using GlowUp, you agree to follow these basic terms.

Account Responsibilities

Keep your account information accurate. Do not share your account with another person. You are responsible for activity on your account.

Customer Responsibilities

Customers should provide accurate booking information, arrive for appointments, cancel when necessary, and communicate respectfully.

Provider Responsibilities

Providers must list accurate services, prices, availability, qualifications, and portfolio information. Providers should communicate professionally and follow safety standards. New service listings are reviewed before they appear to customers. GlowUp may approve compliant listings, request changes with an explanation, or reject listings for prohibited services. Listings returned for changes stay hidden until the provider resubmits them and they are approved. Reviews consider whether the service name, category, description, image, duration, and price are accurate and clear.

Prohibited Activity

Fraud, harassment, fake bookings, fake reviews, misleading listings, and abusive content are not allowed. Reviews and provider replies should relate to the appointment, avoid personal information, harassment, threats, hate, explicit content, and spam, and remain respectful. Honest criticism and low ratings are allowed.

Reviews and Reports

Customers may review completed appointments, and reviews are published when submitted. Customers and providers may report a review or provider reply they believe violates these guidelines. GlowUp may review reported content and hide content that violates these terms. A report does not automatically mean content will be removed.

Account Actions

GlowUp may remove content or suspend accounts that violate these terms or harm the community.
