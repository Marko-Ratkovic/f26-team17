GROW YOUR BEAUTY BUSINESS

Join GlowUp as a provider

Create a provider account, introduce your professional services, and manage customer bookings in one place.

What happens next?

  1. Create an account. Use the shared GlowUp sign-up form and choose Provider.
  2. Complete your professional profile. Add accurate service-area, experience, and credential information.
  3. Submit each service for review. New and revised listings stay hidden until approved.
  4. Manage appointments. Customers can request approved services during the availability you publish.

Be accurate and protect privacy. Only list current credentials you hold. Use a service area rather than a private home address, and do not include customer information in public descriptions.
